08FAQSeen on Harvey · Cal.com · IroncladWording follows the approved claims

Questions a careful buyer asks.

The one worry left at the end of the page, answered in a sentence.

Does Sentinel use a language model?

Not to find or prove a finding. Findings come from a deterministic engine that reads the clauses and plays the contract forward; a language model is used only to talk with you about the results.

How is a finding “proven”?

Each one carries the clause it comes from, the scenario that triggers it, and a replayable trace. You can check every step yourself.

What happens to our contracts?

They stay yours. Sentinel doesn’t train on them unless you switch that on, and it is off by default. Delete a contract and the file, the findings, the memo and every export go with it.

Which formats and languages?

PDF and DOCX in, a memorandum in PDF or DOCX out. The interface is available in English and Russian.

What does the memorandum contain?

Every finding, ranked by exposure, with its citation and a redline. The page, the PDF and the DOCX are the same document.

Can we run it on our own playbook?

Yes. Stance-based redlines and custom playbooks tell the engine which side you are on and what you will not accept.

How do we start?

A 15-minute walkthrough on one of your own agreements. Bring a tricky clause.
